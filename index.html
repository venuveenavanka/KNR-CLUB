Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a member of Karimnagar Club?
Membership at Karimnagar Club is available via invitation or member referral. You can also submit an application via our concierge hotline or by visiting our administration office.
What are the check-in and check-out times for suites?
Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. VIP members enjoy complimentary early check-in and late check-out privileges based on availability.
Are the sports facilities open to room guests?
Yes! All suite and villa guests enjoy complimentary access to the Olympic lap pool, tennis courts, and gym during their stay. Golf green fees and equestrian lessons are available at member rates.
